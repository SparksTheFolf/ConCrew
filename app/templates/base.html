{% if current_user.role == 'admin' %}

You have full control over ConCrew for this convention. Here's what you can do:

  • Set up Convention Settings -- con years, dates, and departments
  • Add Users and set up Reward Tiers
  • Grant Shift Eligibility so volunteers can pick up open shifts
  • Approve Pending Hours and fulfill Reward Claims
  • Send Mass Emails or shift reminders to volunteers
  • Keep an eye on the Admin Dashboard for who's checked in, upcoming shifts, and possible no-shows

Start here: Convention Settings, then Add User.

{% elif current_user.role == 'assigner' %}

You help run volunteers for your assigned department(s). Here's what you can do:

  • View your team on the Roster
  • Log Hours for volunteers and approve Pending Hours
  • Approve or deny shift requests, and assign volunteers directly from the Calendar
  • Grant Shift Eligibility for your departments
  • Review No-Shows for shifts that never got checked into

Start here: the Calendar to see open shifts, or the Roster to see your volunteers.

{% elif current_user.role == 'constore' %}

You help volunteers redeem the rewards they've earned. Here's what you can do:

  • Use Reward Claims to search a volunteer and see what they're eligible for
  • Mark items as fulfilled when you hand them out
  • Ask for their Volunteer Code (or scan their QR code) to find them quickly

Start here: Reward Claims.

{% else %}

Here's how to get set up as a volunteer:

  • Use the Calendar to see open shifts and request the ones you want
  • Mark your Availability on the calendar so staff know when you're free, even without requesting a specific shift
  • Once a shift is confirmed, check in and out from My Progress
  • Can't make a confirmed shift? Use Swap Shifts to trade with another volunteer, or request leave
  • Track your hours and reward tiers on My Progress

Start here: the Calendar, to find your first shift.
